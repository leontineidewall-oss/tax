    FAQ

    Questions

    How is cost basis worked out?

    First in, first out, per token, across every wallet you add. Each buy is valued in SOL (or USDC), then converted to USD at that day's SOL/USD close.

    Which DEXs and bots does it cover?

    All of them. TAX reads how your own balances changed in each transaction, so Pump.fun, Raydium, Jupiter, Meteora, Orca and trading bots are all counted the same way.

    What about airdrops and transfers?

    Tokens that arrive without a payment have no cost basis. If you sell them, they count from zero and are flagged in the CSV. Moving tokens between your own wallets? Add both wallets.

    Who can export?

    Holders of $TAX. Connect the wallet that holds it.

    Do you keep my wallets?

    No. Scans run in your browser. Nothing is saved, and there are no accounts.

    Is this tax advice?

    No. It is the math from on-chain data. Your accountant still files.
